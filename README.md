# Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Business Analysis

This project presents an end-to-end business data analysis of the **Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Dataset**, focusing on sales performance, customer purchasing behaviour, product categories, payment preferences, geographical trends, and delivery operations.

The analysis covers **113,360 product transactions across 94,720 completed orders**, with reported product sales of approximately **R$13.62 million** and freight charges of **R$2.27 million**.

### Key Areas of Analysis

- **Sales Performance:** Analysed total revenue, average order value (AOV), monthly sales trends, and year-over-year growth.
- **Product Category Analysis:** Identified top revenue-generating categories, highest-selling products by volume, and high-value categories.
- **Geographical Analysis:** Evaluated state-wise sales distribution, identified major customer markets, and measured revenue concentration.
- **Payment Analysis:** Compared credit cards, debit cards, Boleto, and vouchers based on transaction volume and revenue contribution.
- **Delivery Performance:** Calculated delivery time, compared estimated and actual delivery dates, and classified orders as Early, On Time, or Late.
- **Logistics Analysis:** Identified regions with higher delivery times, freight costs, and late-delivery rates.

### Key Business Insights

- Health & Beauty emerged as the leading category by product revenue.
- São Paulo contributed approximately 38.43% of total product revenue.
- Credit cards accounted for 73.82% of payment transactions.
- Approximately 91.90% of delivered orders arrived before their estimated delivery dates.
- Alagoas showed significant delivery challenges, with a reported late-delivery rate of 23.92%.
- Average basket size was approximately 1.20 items per order, indicating opportunities for cross-selling.

### Business Recommendations

The project proposes data-driven strategies, including optimising delivery estimates, improving regional logistics, testing free-shipping thresholds, implementing product recommendations, and evaluating faster payment methods.

### Project Objective

The primary objective is to transform raw e-commerce data into meaningful business insights and actionable recommendations. This project demonstrates the application of Python-based data analysis to real-world business problems, combining technical skills with analytical thinking and business decision-making.

**Note:** All reported findings are based on the analysed dataset and should be validated against the original transaction data before operational implementation.
